# Metro City Transit Authority Announces Fare-Free Weekend Bus Policy

Metro City Mayor Sarah Jenkins and Transit Authority Director Mark Roberts announced today that all city buses will be completely free of charge on Saturdays and Sundays starting next month.

The 6-month pilot program aims to reduce traffic congestion, encourage public transit usage, and provide economic relief to local families. City Council member David Chen voiced support, citing potential environmental benefits, while Local Business Coalition president Elena Vance praised the initiative for increasing foot traffic to downtown shops. 

However, Taxpayers Alliance spokesperson Tom Bradley expressed concern regarding budget allocation, arguing the estimated $4.2 million shortfall might eventually lead to higher municipal property taxes.
